# Browser Control MCP: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Browser Control MCP is a Chrome extension that lets AI tools running on your own computer control browser tabs through a local MCP server.

## What the extension handles
When your local MCP server asks it to, the extension reads the content, URL and title of the tabs it controls, takes screenshots of them, and can read their console and network events. It stores your settings (such as the local port, site permissions, input lock and hidden-text filter preferences, recipe pacing defaults, per-host pacing numbers without page text, and the local connection token) in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Nothing is synced to the cloud.

## Where that data goes
All of it goes only to the MCP server on your own computer, over a local connection (127.0.0.1). The extension does not send any data to the developer or to any other remote server, contains no analytics or tracking, and loads no remote code. What your local AI tool does with the data it receives is governed by that tool's own terms.

## Sharing and sale
We do not collect, sell, share or transfer user data. We do not use data for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose.

## Retention and control
Settings stay on your device until you change them or remove the extension. Removing the extension deletes its local storage. You can pause, lock/unlock, or disconnect the agent at any time from the extension's popup or tab control bar.
